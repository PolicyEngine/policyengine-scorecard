# Tax Policy Associates — Autumn Budget 2025 harvest (source `tax_policy_associates`)

Staged 2026-09-24 from the primary documents. **3008 claims** in `claims_staged.jsonl.gz`, **7 documents** in `manifest.jsonl`. Row contract, enums and rules: `../README.md`; measure keys: `../REGISTRY_KEYS.md`. Every `value` is verbatim (normalised to the unit as recorded in `normalization`); nothing is derived.

## Access recipe

All four articles fetched directly (`curl`, browser User-Agent, HTTP 200; `article:published_time` meta used for
`date`). The two interactive artefacts were fetched from the same site:
- council tax calculator: `https://taxpolicy.org.uk/wp-content/assets/council_tax_calculator_2/council_tax_webapp.html`
  and its `council_tax_webapp.js` beside it (band counts are constants in the JS; code mirrored at
  github.com/DanNeidle/council_tax_webapp_nov_2025);
- mansion tax map data: `https://taxpolicy.org.uk/wp-content/assets/council_tax_data/` → `map_data_manifest.json`,
  `constituency_council_tax_bands.geojson` (14.3 MB, 543 English constituencies with £2m+ transaction counts by
  bracket) and `mansion_tax_treemap.json` (276 constituencies with "Estimated mansion tax liability", £m; generated by
  `3_treemap_generator.py` in github.com/DanNeidle/mansion_tax_estimate_and_webapp). `doc_type` for these is recorded
  as `flourish_json` (the nearest enum for a chart-data endpoint), `access: direct`.
The seed's "constituency values live only in the interactive map" was therefore resolved: the values are published as
JSON data files and staged in full. The web app computes per-constituency revenue client-side as counts × £2,500 /
£3,500 / £5,000 / £7,500 (app.js `SURCHARGE_RATE_MAP`); those computed map figures are NOT staged (derivation), only
the treemap's published liability values and the GeoJSON's published counts are.

  Band H; "roughly 80%" from Band G; "an average rise of around £4,000" per Band G household; Band G 888,000 and

  £1.5m" (same as the 154,000 constant) — ratios/duplicates.

  page this sentence appears only inside a **reader comment quoting HMT guidance**, not in TPA's article → not TPA's,

  effective and net-vs-gross curves for employment, self-employment, partnership/LLP, IR35 or retired income; 2025/26

  `conditions.geography_level: westminster_constituency` on constituency rows. Producer definition (27 Nov): "our
  approach is likely generating a lower-bound estimate of the actual static revenue from the tax. The total estimated

  of around £4,000".

  `proposed_metric: affected_share` (`share`) for 0.6% / 3.5%.

  (`conditions.value_band`: "£2m and over (all brackets)", "£2m to £2.5m", "£2.5m to £3.5m", "£3.5m to £5m", "over
  £5m"). Producer definition: "use the Land Registry price paid database to find every property transaction since
