# Autumn Budget 2025: scores published by other shops (own computations only)

Compiled 24 September 2026 from the primary documents (PDFs downloaded and text-extracted; HTML pages fetched). Every number is as printed by the publisher. Conventions:

- Tags: `[ANNOUNCED]` = an Autumn Budget 2025 (26 Nov 2025) measure; `[OPTION]` = a pre-Budget option or alternative costed for that Budget (Sept–Nov 2025) or a post-Budget alternative; `[CONTEXT]` = the organisation's own number that is neither (living-standards benchmarks, post-Budget non-Budget analysis). Restated OBR/HMT/DWP figures are listed separately under "Restated (excluded from main list)" so they are not mistaken for own computations.
- Field order in every bullet: `[tag] measure | metric type | value(s) with units and years | basis | model/data | publication, date | URL`.
- "approx." marks a chart reading or a rounded figure quoted by the publisher; "chart only" means the value exists only in a figure and is not stated in the text.
