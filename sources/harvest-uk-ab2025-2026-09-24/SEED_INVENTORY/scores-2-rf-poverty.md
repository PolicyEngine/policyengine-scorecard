# Autumn Budget 2025 scores: Resolution Foundation, JRF, IPPR, CPAG, Policy in Practice, entitledto, Trussell

Compiled 24 Sep 2026 from the primary PDFs and pages (all fetched and read; image-only tables rasterised and read). Conventions: `[ANNOUNCED]` = an Autumn Budget 2025 measure, or a measure in force by April 2026 that the organisation scored as part of the Budget package (UC standard-allowance over-indexation and the UC-health cut were legislated in the Universal Credit Act 2025 before the Budget and are flagged as such); `[OPTION]` = a pre-Budget option, alternative or counterfactual. `RESTATED` marks a figure the organisation repeats from OBR/HMT/DWP/government rather than computing itself. Basis abbreviations: AHC = after housing costs; BHC = before housing costs; eq. = equivalised; HBAI = DWP Households Below Average Income; IPPR TBM = IPPR Tax-Benefit Model (static, FRS-based); "approx." = read from a chart.
