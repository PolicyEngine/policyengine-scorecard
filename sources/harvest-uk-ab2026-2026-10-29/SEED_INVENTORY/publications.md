# Autumn Budget 2026: publications to harvest

Compiled 29 October 2026 from the reaction monitor that ran from 28 October 19:05Z to 29 October 14:05Z. Each entry names a publication the monitor read and the kinds of figure it holds. Figures are paraphrased here; the harvest reads each primary itself and stages only what the primary prints. Times are UTC.

## HM Treasury (28 October, 13:30Z)

- Budget 2026 document, PDF and HTML.
  - PDF: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ae20b85460c1346f7be784e/E03668986_HMT_Budget_2026_ELAY__2_.pdf
  - The PDF was replaced after 28 October: the 28 October download hashes to bed70546…, the 29 October download to 19b337ac….
  - Holds measure descriptions with numbers, for example the Household Energy Payment's "around 20 million households".
- Supporting documents (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/supporting-documents-for-budget-2026):
  - Table 6.1 and 6.2 (staged in `uk_hmt_tables`);
  - Table 6.4 recostings (staged);
  - Policy costings (AB26_Policy_Costing.pdf);
  - Impact on households;
  - Data Sources.
- Pension Tax Allowance explainer (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/pension-tax-allowance-explainer/pension-tax-allowance-explainer). Holds counts of pensioners who gain and who pay no tax, plus worked cases.

## OBR (28 October)

- Economic and fiscal outlook, October 2026, with its charts-and-tables workbooks and the detailed forecast tables (the policy workbook).
  - Chapter 3 costs each measure and states the behavioural adjustments, for example paragraph 3.17 on the savings and property income tax rises.
  - The fuel duty path is in paragraph 3.19.

## Resolution Foundation

- Press release, 29 October, 06:00Z.
- *Spread thin*, published 29 October at 08:20Z: https://www.resolutionfoundation.org/publications/spread-thin/ (PDF …/app/uploads/2026/10/Spread-thin-1.pdf).
